How do I dictate emails in new Outlook?
How do I use Dictation in Outlook?
Simplify how I can dictate my emails with Copilot.
Dictation in Outlook lets you use speech-to-text to author content with a microphone and reliable internet connection. It's a quick and easy way to draft emails, send replies, and capture the tone you're going for.

Privacy
This service doesn't store your audio data or transcribed text.
Your speech utterances are sent to Microsoft and used only to provide you with text results.
For more information about experiences that analyze your content, see
Connected Experiences in Office
.
Troubleshooting
Microphone problems
If you see "Another app is using your mic":
Make sure no other application or web page is using the microphone and try again.
If you see "Mic issue", "Can't detect mic", or "Can't access mic":
Make sure the microphone is plugged in.
Test the microphone to make sure it's working.
Check the microphone settings in the
Control Panel
.
Also, see
How to set up and test microphones in Windows 10
.
On a Surface running Windows 10:
Adjust microphone settings
.
Also, see
Troubleshoot microphone issues in Microsoft 365 for the web
.
Dictation can't hear you
: If nothing appears on the screen as you dictate:
Make sure your microphone isn't muted.
Adjust the input level of your microphone.
Move to a quieter location.
If you're using a built-in mic, consider trying again with a headset or external mic.
Accuracy problems or missed words
: If you see a lot of incorrect words or missed words:
Make sure you're on a fast and reliable internet connection.
Avoid or eliminate background noise that might interfere with your voice.
Try speaking more deliberately.
Check to see if the microphone you're using needs to be upgraded.
