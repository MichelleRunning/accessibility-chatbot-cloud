Listen to your Word documents with Read Aloud
Read Aloud
highlights each word as it reads your document to you. Use Read Aloud to hear your document narrated to you or to support your understanding when reading any Word document.
Open a document.
Click or tap where you would like Read Aloud to begin playing.
Place your cursor at the beginning of the document to read the whole document aloud.
