Open, read, and edit calendar events
To keep up with your busy schedule, you can quickly read and edit event details.
Note
You can only edit events that you've created. For example, you cannot edit meeting requests that you've received from someone else.
In Outlook, press
Ctrl
+
2
to open the
Calendar
.
Do one of the following to move in the calendar:
To move to the previous day, week, or month, press the
Tab
key until you hear "Go to previous," followed by the unit of time, and then press
Enter
.
To move to the next day, week, or month, press the
Tab
key until you hear "Go to next," followed by the unit of time, and then press
Enter
.
In any view, press the
Tab
key or
Shift
+
Tab
to browse the events for the time period displayed. As you move, you'll hear "Event title," followed by the time, date, event location, and organizer of the selected event.
To review the event in more detail, or open the event to edit it, press
Enter
. The event
Details
window opens.
To browse the event details and options, press the
Tab
key. Your screen reader announces the details and options as you move.
When finished, do one of the following:
If you are editing a meeting, press
Shift
+
Tab
until you hear "Send button," and then press
Enter
.
If you are editing an appointment, press
Shift
+
Tab
until you hear "Save button," and then press
Spacebar
.
If you didn't change anything in the event, to close the event
Details
window, press
Esc
.
When you leave the
Details
window, the focus moves to
Calendar
.

Set up your calendar view
You can choose between three options to view the calendar:
Agenda
,
Day
,
3-Day
, or
Month
view. The default option is
Agenda
.
In the Outlook for iOS calendar, tap near the top of the screen with four fingers, flick right until you hear "Change view," followed by the current view, for example, "Agenda view," and then double-tap the screen.
A list of the view options opens. Flick right or left until you hear the option you want, and then double-tap the screen.
Open, read, and edit calendar events
To keep up with your busy schedule, you can quickly read and edit event details.
Note
You can only edit events that you've created. For example, you cannot edit meeting requests that you've received from someone else.
In the Outlook for iOS calendar, flick right until you hear "Change view," and do one of the following:
In the
Agenda
view, flick right until you hear "Agenda." To browse to events in the future, flick up.
In the
Day
view, flick right until you hear "Day today," followed by the current date and the number of events scheduled for today. To review events in the coming days, flick up.
In the
3-Day
view, flick right until you hear "Timeslot events," followed by the details of the first event of the day. To browse to events in the future, flick up.
In the
Month
view, flick right until you hear the first event in the current month's view. To browse to events in the future, flick right. If you have multiple entries for a single day, double-tap the screen to switch to the
Agenda
view and review each event separately.
When you're on an event whose details you want to review, double-tap the screen. The event details open in a new window.
To hear the event details, flick right or left or slide one finger down the screen. VoiceOver announces the details as you move. To close the details window without editing the details, flick left until you hear "Back button," and double-tap the screen.
To edit the event, in the event details window, flick right or left until you hear "Edit," and double-tap the screen. The
Edit Event
window opens.
Flick right until you hear the event detail you want to edit, double-tap the screen, and make the changes you want.
When finished, to save your changes, flick left until you hear "Done," and double-tap the screen. The focus returns to the event details window.
To close the event details window, flick left until you hear "Back button," and double-tap the screen. The focus returns to the calendar grid.

Note
To join a Microsoft Teams meeting as described below, make sure you have the Microsoft Teams app installed on your phone, the app is running, and you've signed in to Microsoft Teams with your work or school account.
In your Outlook for iOS calendar, move the focus to the event you want to join. To open the meeting details window, double-tap the screen.
Flick right until you hear "Join button," and double-tap the screen.
The Microsoft Teams app opens, and you're connected to the meeting.

Respond to event reminders
When you have events scheduled in the Outlook app and you have turned on notifications, VoiceOver announces reminders of events at the predefined time prior to the event and an event reminder pops up on the screen. The focus moves to the reminder.
To respond to the reminder, do one of the following:
To open the event details, flick right until you hear "View," and double-tap the screen.
To close the reminder until the event time, flick right until you hear "Snooze," and double-tap the screen.
To close the reminder without taking any action, flick right until you hear "Close," and double-tap the screen.

Open, read, and edit calendar events
To keep up with your busy schedule, you can quickly read and edit event details.
Note
You can only edit events that you've created. You can't edit meeting requests that you've received from someone else.
In the Outlook for Android calendar, slide one finger across the screen until you hear the title of the event you want to review, and double-tap the screen. The event details window opens.
Tip
If you have many events in your calendar, you can browse them by swiping up and down with two fingers.
To hear the event title, day, date, time, location, and other information, flick right or left or slide one finger down the screen. TalkBack announces the details as you move. To close the details window without editing the details, flick down-then-left.
To edit the event, in the event details window, flick right or left until you hear "Edit button," and double-tap the screen. The
Edit Event
window opens.
Flick right until you hear the event detail you want to edit, and then double-tap the screen. Make the changes you want. When finished, flick left until you hear "Save," and double-tap the screen.
Tip
To cancel the event, flick right until you hear "Cancel event," and double-tap the screen.
To close the event details window, flick left until you hear "Back button," and then double-tap the screen.

Note

In the Outlook for Android calendar, move the focus to the event you want to join. When an event is a Microsoft Teams meeting, TalkBack announces "At location Microsoft Teams meeting." To open the meeting details window, double-tap the screen.

Use a screen reader to join a meeting in Microsoft Teams

Respond to event reminders
If you have notifications turned on for your Outlook Calendar events, you hear a chime at the predefined time prior to the event.
To bring up the event reminder, flick down from the top of the screen with two fingers to open the notification shade.
Flick right until you hear "Outlook," followed by the event details.
Do one of the following:
To open the event details window to review the details, double-tap the screen.
To close the reminder without taking any action, double-tap and hold the screen, flick right until you hear "Done," and double-tap the screen.

Technical support for customers with disabilities
Microsoft wants to provide the best possible experience for all our customers. If you have a disability or questions related to accessibility, please contact the
Microsoft Disability Answer Desk
for technical assistance. The Disability Answer Desk support team is trained in using many popular assistive technologies and can offer assistance in English, Spanish, French, and American Sign Language. Please go to the Microsoft Disability Answer Desk site to find out the contact details for your region.
If you are a government, commercial, or enterprise user, please contact
